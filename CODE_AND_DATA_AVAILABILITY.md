# Code and Data Availability

## Manuscript-ready English text

Source code, frozen inference configurations, checkpoint manifests and scripts required to reproduce the reported analyses are available at https://github.com/951209XN/RimBind-. Frozen task-specific checkpoints, derived training-memory labels and figure-ready result tables will be deposited in Zenodo before submission; the DOI will be inserted in the final manuscript and repository release. Source structures and metadata are available from the RCSB Protein Data Bank under their original accessions. Predicted monomer structures can be regenerated from the released sequence manifests and frozen ColabFold protocol. SaProt and ESM3 foundation-model weights are not redistributed and must be obtained from their official repositories under the corresponding licenses.

## Submission gate

Do not retain the future-tense Zenodo sentence in a submitted manuscript. Upload the archive, mint a DOI, replace the placeholder, and test the DOI from a clean browser session first.
